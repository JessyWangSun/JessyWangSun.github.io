👋 About Me

Hi! I’m Jessy, a Ph.D. candidate in Emerging Media Studies at Boston University, co-advised through the Personal Robots Group at the MIT Media Lab.

My research investigates how people can benefit from conversational AI while mitigating potential risks of dependency, particularly in relation to psychological well-being and cognitive agency.

I bridge behavioral science and technical AI evaluation by translating theories of support, reliance, and well-being into measurable signals, annotation frameworks, evaluation pipelines, and safer AI interventions for youth and other vulnerable populations.

  • AI Dependency, Overreliance, and Cognitive Agency
  • Psychological Well-Being and AI-Mediated Social Support
