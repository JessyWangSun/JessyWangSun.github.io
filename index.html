👋 About Me

Hi! I’m Jessy, a Ph.D. candidate in Emerging Media Studies at Boston University, co-advised through the Personal Robots Group at the MIT Media Lab. I study how people rely on and build relationships with conversational AI.

My work sits at the intersection of Human-AI interaction, child safety, content moderation, and youth well-being. I translate behavioral science into measurable AI-safety signals, build evaluation and moderation pipelines, and co-design developmentally appropriate AI experiences with the people they are intended to support.
